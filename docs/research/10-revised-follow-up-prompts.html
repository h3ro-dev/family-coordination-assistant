Prompt A: 30-Day Paid Pilot And User Research Design

Act as a startup product strategist, user researcher, and pricing experimentation lead.

Use the attached research packet first. The packet contains the prior Looheru research, Friday meeting notes, and GPT 5.5 Pro reports on monetization, COGS, competition, AI voice risk, and partnership structures.

Goal:
Design the next 30-day paid pilot for Looheru so we can validate willingness to pay, repeat usage, operational cost, and founder-led GTM before the team overbuilds.

Current strategic context:
- Looheru is best positioned as execution-first family coordination, not a calendar, reminder app, marketplace, or generic AI assistant.
- Highest-confidence launch wedge is saved-contact sitter or trusted-contact coordination by SMS/email.
- Healthcare/provider voice is strategically attractive but legally and operationally higher risk and should be excluded or human-reviewed in MVP.
- Candidate pricing is Essential $29/month, Plus $59/month, Signature $119/month, with task credits and fair-use caps.
- First likely ICP is high-income, high-burden working mothers, especially physician mothers and dual-career households.

Please produce:
1. The exact pilot thesis.
2. Target participant profile and exclusion criteria.
3. Recruiting script for Tamara's network.
4. Screening questions.
5. Paid offer cards for $29, $59, and $119.
6. What task types are allowed in the pilot and what is out of scope.
7. Onboarding flow.
8. First-week user journey.
9. Metrics dashboard: activation, completed tasks, repeat usage, time-to-confirm, COGS, support minutes, NPS/relief, conversion.
10. Interview script before use, after first completed task, and at day 30.
11. Success thresholds for proceeding, pivoting, or stopping.
12. What data to collect without storing sensitive child/health content.
13. How to turn pilot results into a sponsored cohort pitch.

Be concrete enough that we could run the pilot with 15-25 families in June 2026.
