Current Market Validation And Option Scope

Date: 2026-05-12 Trace ID: trc_20260512_011043Z_vu25596g

Executive Update

The prior A/B/C option ranges were directionally useful but under-specified. They did not make clear enough what was included in each option, and the cash ranges for Options A and B were too light if the engagement includes production hardening, autonomous onboarding, Stripe, task-level instrumentation, pilot dashboards, voice policy gates, and partner-level operating accountability.

The revised recommendation:

Keep three options, but separate them by scope and risk. Option B remains the recommended structure, but the cash range should increase and the equity range should be tied directly to measurable delivery milestones. Option C should be reserved for a true cofounder/studio role and should carry materially higher equity than the earlier draft.

Revised Option A: Cash Services / No Equity

Best for maximum founder ownership and clean vendor economics.

Economics

  • Discovery / solution map: $15K-$25K.
  • 6-8 week Build + Own Sprint: $85K-$140K.
  • Follow-on product build: $150K-$350K+, depending on scope.
  • Post-sprint maintenance / delivery retainer: $20K-$45K/month.
  • Equity: 0%.

Included In Build + Own Sprint

  • Repo/deployment/account audit.
  • Entity/IP/account ownership checklist.
  • Auth and admin hardening plan.
  • Twilio webhook signature verification.
  • Twilio Lookup / line-type gate design.
  • Stripe checkout and plan entitlement design.
  • Autonomous onboarding flow for household, contacts, task setup, consent, and first task.
  • Task-level COGS ledger and event taxonomy.
  • Pilot dashboard for activation, completion, COGS, task status, and handoffs.
  • Public-safe pilot runbook and launch checklist.
  • Legal-informed voice/data policy matrix for counsel validation.

Excluded Unless Added To SOW

  • Realtime voice bridge.
  • Native iOS/Android apps.
  • Full HIPAA/SOC 2 program.
  • Insurance/prior authorization workflows.
  • Healthcare-provider-side PHI workflows.
  • Calendar/contact deep OAuth integrations beyond scoped pilot needs.
  • 24/7 support or human concierge operations.
  • Fundraising, legal entity formation, or legal document drafting.

Revised Option B: Strategic Build Partner

Recommended if the relationship is intended to be more than vendor services.

Economics

  • 6-8 week Build + Own Sprint cash: $50K-$90K.
  • Post-sprint operator/build retainer: $15K-$30K/month.
  • Equity or warrant pool: 5%-10%, committed up front but vested over 24-36 months and milestone gates.

Why This Changed

The earlier $25K-$45K sprint cash plus 3%-7% equity was likely too low if Utlyze / SolutionStream is expected to own product architecture, production hardening, AI voice strategy, GTM instrumentation, and operating cadence. If the cash is meaningfully discounted from market services, the equity should compensate for the risk and responsibility.

Included

Everything in Option A, plus:

  • Strategic product ownership with Tamara.
  • Technical roadmap ownership.
  • Architecture and vendor decision memos.
  • Sprint operating cadence and release discipline.
  • Partner participation in paid pilot review.
  • Voice-roadmap technical decisioning.
  • Post-sprint recommendation on whether to scale, pivot, pause, or deepen partnership.

Equity Vesting Proposal

  • 1%-2%: production hardening, account ownership, pilot launch readiness.
  • 1%-2%: autonomous onboarding, Stripe/plan caps, task instrumentation, pilot dashboard.
  • 1%-2%: paid pilot execution support and evidence packet.
  • 1%-2%: scripted voice hardening and voice/data policy gates.
  • Remaining tranche, if any: sponsored cohort readiness or realtime voice bridge decision package.

Unvested equity stops if the relationship ends. Fully vested day-one equity is not recommended.

Revised Option C: Venture Studio / Cofounder-Level Build Partner

Best only if Utlyze / SolutionStream is expected to be a true company-building partner, not a vendor.

Economics

  • Cash: $0K-$35K/month, depending on how much of the team cost is subsidized.
  • Equity: 15%-30% earned equity from the current product baseline.
  • Higher than 30% only if Utlyze / SolutionStream contributes material capital, a sustained full-stack team, executive operating responsibility, hiring/fundraising support, and accepts studio-level company-creation risk.

Included

  • Everything in Option B.
  • Sustained product/engineering leadership.
  • Dedicated or recurring engineering capacity.
  • Fundraising / investor diligence support.
  • Sponsor/cohort sales support.
  • Hiring roadmap and vendor management.
  • Realtime voice bridge build decision and implementation if approved.
  • Longer-term operating cadence through pilot, sponsored cohort, and next financing/scale decision.

Required Governance

  • Founder-control reserved matters.
  • Board or observer rights only if scope justifies them.
  • Vesting, cliff, milestone, and repurchase terms.
  • Background IP carveout.
  • Company-owned venture-specific IP.
  • Budget authority and spending rules.
  • Offboarding and transition rights.
  • Cap-table impact modeled before signature.

Additional Research Questions Needed

Scope And Build Cost

  1. Which systems are already production-ready versus prototype-only?
  2. What must be built in the first 6-8 weeks versus deferred?
  3. Is calendar/contact import part of the first paid pilot or later?
  4. Is parent-facing voice intake in the first sprint or a separate voice sprint?
  5. Is the goal pilot-readiness, paid launch, or sponsor-readiness?

Pricing And Equity

  1. What cash budget can Tamara commit without outside financing?
  2. What level of equity dilution is acceptable before paid pilot proof?
  3. Does Utlyze / SolutionStream expect discounted cash, market cash, or mostly equity?
  4. What valuation or implied valuation should be used if warrants/equity are granted?
  5. Should equity vest by time, milestone, or a hybrid?

Responsibility

  1. Who owns production incidents during the pilot?
  2. Who owns user support, failed tasks, refunds, and exception handling?
  3. Who owns vendor accounts and billing?
  4. Who has product decision rights when speed and safety conflict?
  5. What does "operator" mean in practice: architecture only, delivery leadership, GTM support, hiring, fundraising, or all of the above?

Legal / Compliance

  1. Which states are in the first pilot?
  2. What data classes are explicitly prohibited?
  3. Will the pilot include provider calls, or only saved-contact family logistics?
  4. What legal review is needed before scripted voice, realtime voice, or healthcare-adjacent tasks?
  5. What retention policy is acceptable for message and voice-derived data?
