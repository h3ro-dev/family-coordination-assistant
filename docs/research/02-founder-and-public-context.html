Founder And Public Context

Verified Public Role

Public sources validate that Tamara Moores Todd, MD is a senior digital health and informatics leader at Intermountain Health.

The Intermountain newsroom announced on May 9, 2025 that she would serve as Chief Health Informatics Officer for the system. The same source says she draws on more than a decade of clinical and administrative experience, led initiatives including advanced decision support and clinical communication ecosystems, and is board certified in emergency medicine and clinical informatics.

Source: https://news.intermountainhealth.org/intermountain-health-names-new-chief-clinical-information-officer-and-chief-health-informatics-officer/

AI / Trust / Governance Positioning

Public coverage of her AI Agent Behavioral Science Conference keynote positions her as governance- and trust-oriented rather than hype-oriented. TechBuzz reported that her keynote centered on accountability, trust, policy, compliance, risk comfort, and safe failure modes for healthcare AI.

Source: https://www.techbuzznews.com/why-healthcare-ai-fails-without-accountability/

HealthLeaders' HIMSS coverage also quotes her as starting AI deployments with lower-risk operational friction points before moving into complex clinical use cases. That maps well to Looheru's stated first phase: start with family logistics, not medical decision-making.

Source: https://www.healthleadersmedia.com/technology/himss26-4-strategies-hospital-leaders-are-deploying-scale-ai

Fit For Looheru

The founder-market fit looks strong on three axes:

  • Lived problem: mother of three, including a child with special needs, describing real coordination load.
  • Domain authority: clinical informatics executive who understands high-stakes coordination systems.
  • Trust posture: public messaging around governance, risk, and responsible AI aligns with the privacy-sensitive nature of family and caregiving data.

Potential Founder Constraints

The same public credibility creates constraints that should be clarified before partnership:

  • Employer conflict policy: whether an Intermountain executive can operate a consumer AI caregiving company on the side.
  • Healthcare boundary: whether healthcare scheduling or clinic calls create HIPAA, medical advice, or provider-representation exposure.
  • Brand separation: whether Intermountain identity can be referenced in GTM or should remain only biographical.
  • Time allocation: whether founder attention is sufficient to move from prototype to customer learning, sales, support, and operations.

Company Public Presence

https://looheru.ai is live but currently only returns a Squarespace under-construction page. The page includes noindex, so it is not positioned for public discovery yet. There is no public product copy, pricing, privacy policy, terms, waitlist, or demo flow visible from the site.

That is not a problem for a prelaunch company, but it means the email is currently the primary source of truth.
