Market-Grounded Partnership And Technology Options

Date: 2026-05-11 Trace ID: trc_20260511_235419Z_5u96wlf5

Executive Update

The prior proposal was too conservative in two ways:

  1. It treated legal review as a gating blocker instead of a risk-management validation step.
  2. It treated upfront equity as something to avoid, when a serious technical/build partner would reasonably want equity if it is taking real product, architecture, implementation, and operating risk.

The corrected stance:

Looheru should move forward with research-backed product gates, not legal paralysis. Counsel should validate the highest-risk edges, but the team can make informed product decisions from the available regulatory, vendor, and market data. Utlyze/SolutionStream should also present equity-bearing paths because a company-building technical partner is not the same as an advisor or hourly vendor.

Repo-Grounded Product Baseline

The associated product repo is not just a concept deck. It is a working coordination engine:

  • Repo: h3ro-dev/family-coordination-assistant
  • Local checkout: [local path redacted]
  • Stack: Node, Fastify, TypeScript, Postgres, pg-boss, Twilio, Resend.
  • Current channels: SMS, email, and Twilio Voice scripted calling.
  • Current workflow engine: request -> outreach -> options -> parent choice -> confirmation.
  • Existing voice components:
  • voice_jobs table;
  • TwilioVoiceDialer;
  • /webhooks/twilio/voice/answer;
  • /webhooks/twilio/voice/gather;
  • /webhooks/twilio/voice/status;
  • /webhooks/voice/result;
  • clinic / therapy task creation in admin UI;
  • scripted availability and booking calls;
  • voice-result ingestion into task_options.

The repo already proves the core architecture: a durable coordination core with replaceable adapters. The next technology step is hardening and upgrading, not inventing the first voice path from scratch.

Legal-Informed, Not Legal-Blocked

The product can responsibly move faster if we distinguish:

  • legal facts that are already knowable;
  • product risk gates we can encode;
  • residual legal questions that should be validated before scale.

Known enough to design around:

  • FCC: AI-generated humanlike voices are treated as artificial/prerecorded voice under TCPA analysis.
  • Twilio Lookup: line-type intelligence can identify mobile, landline, fixed VoIP, non-fixed VoIP, toll-free, and more.
  • California AB 3030: appointment scheduling, billing, and other clerical/business matters are expressly carved out from patient clinical information in that statute.
  • HHS: parent/personal-representative authority is usually broad for minors, but exceptions exist for minor-confidential services and state-law specific situations.
  • FTC: non-HIPAA consumer health apps can still face health-data breach and unauthorized-disclosure obligations.
  • Twilio: Programmable Voice/SMS can support HIPAA-eligible workflows under the right account, product, and BAA structure if Looheru later crosses into PHI-heavy scope.

Recommended operating stance:

  • Phase 1 and Phase 2 can proceed from researched product gates.
  • Counsel should review the exact policy matrix before broad scale, state expansion, PHI-heavy workflows, or clinical/identity/minor-confidential categories.
  • Counsel should not be framed as the reason the team cannot build or test. Counsel validates and sharpens the launch rules.

Technology Roadmap By Phase

Phase 0: Own And Harden The Current Product

Use the existing repo.

Build:

  • company-owned repo/domain/Twilio/Resend/Railway/Stripe controls;
  • Twilio webhook signature verification;
  • Twilio Lookup line-type checks;
  • Stripe payment and plan caps;
  • autonomous onboarding;
  • task ledger and COGS tracking;
  • structured failure / blocked-task taxonomy;
  • retention changes so voice stores structured results, not raw transcripts by default.

Phase 1: Paid Autonomous SMS/Email Coordination

Use:

  • current Fastify/Postgres coordination core;
  • Twilio SMS;
  • Resend email;
  • pg-boss background jobs;
  • current saved-contact state machine.

Goal:

  • prove paid demand and repeat use without Looheru human labor.

Phase 1.5: Parent-Facing Voice Intake

Use:

  • OpenAI Realtime / GPT-Realtime-2 for speech-to-structured task capture;
  • xAI Grok Voice or STT as an alternate adapter;
  • Gemini Live if multimodal Google ecosystem fit matters;
  • the existing tasks, contacts, and task_options model.

Goal:

  • make onboarding and task entry easier for parents before doing broad outbound calls.

Phase 2: Scripted Outbound Voice For Low-Risk Business Logistics

Use and harden the current Twilio Voice implementation:

  • TwiML <Gather input="speech">;
  • deterministic slot parsing;
  • yes/no confirmation;
  • voice_jobs;
  • /webhooks/voice/result;
  • no recording;
  • no voicemail drop;
  • line-type blocklist;
  • business-main-line only;
  • office opt-out.

Goal:

  • use voice for camps, classes, household services, and nonclinical office logistics.

Phase 3: Realtime Voice Bridge

Build a separate voice bridge service only after the scripted lane is proven.

Use:

  • Twilio Media Streams, SIP, or a managed voice-agent layer;
  • OpenAI Realtime / xAI Voice / Gemini Live adapter interface;
  • deterministic tool calls into the coordination core;
  • structured result contract back to /webhooks/voice/result;
  • prohibited-topic classifier;
  • real-time stop rules;
  • structured output only.

Goal:

  • improve conversation flexibility without putting the state machine inside the voice model.

Phase 4: Healthcare Logistics And Sponsored Cohorts

Use:

  • the same core/adapters pattern;
  • Twilio HIPAA-eligible product posture if PHI workflows become real;
  • BAAs only where the product crosses into covered-entity/business-associate or PHI-heavy territory;
  • sponsor cohort dashboards and reporting.

Goal:

  • expand from nonclinical logistics into higher-value healthcare-adjacent coordination with explicit risk acceptance and technical controls.

Market-Grounded Partnership Options

The proposal should give three paths.

Option A: Cash Services / No Equity

Best if Tamara wants maximum founder ownership and treats Utlyze/SolutionStream as a vendor.

Suggested economics:

  • Discovery / solution map: $10K-$18K.
  • Build + Own Sprint: $45K-$75K.
  • Follow-on build: $80K-$180K depending on scope.
  • Equity: 0%.

Trade-off:

  • Lowest dilution.
  • Highest cash requirement.
  • Less upside alignment for Utlyze/SolutionStream.

Option B: Strategic Build Partner

Recommended.

Best if both sides want real alignment without jumping to a full venture-studio structure.

Suggested economics:

  • Build + Own Sprint cash: $25K-$45K.
  • Monthly build/operator retainer after sprint: $8K-$18K/month.
  • Equity or warrant pool: 3%-7%, signed at the start but vesting over time and milestones.

Suggested vesting:

  • 24-36 month vesting.
  • Milestone tranche for production hardening and paid pilot readiness.
  • Milestone tranche for successful paid pilot and autonomous onboarding.
  • Milestone tranche for voice lane hardening and sponsored cohort readiness.
  • Unvested equity stops if the relationship ends.

Trade-off:

  • Preserves founder control.
  • Gives Utlyze/SolutionStream real company upside.
  • More market-consistent than advisor equity because the role is build/operator, not advice.

Option C: Venture Studio / Cofounder-Level Build Partner

Best only if Tamara wants Utlyze/SolutionStream to function as a true co-builder with sustained team, capital, architecture, product, and operating responsibility.

Suggested economics:

  • Cash: $0K-$25K/month depending on how much studio cost is subsidized.
  • Equity: 10%-20% for a meaningful co-build role from the current product baseline.
  • Higher than 20% only if Utlyze/SolutionStream supplies meaningful capital, sustained full-stack team, executive operating responsibility, and accepts company-creation risk comparable to a venture studio.

Trade-off:

  • Highest alignment and lowest cash.
  • Highest dilution.
  • Requires clearer governance, board/observer rights, decision rights, vesting, repurchase/offboarding, and reserved matters.

Market Anchors

Advisor equity is not the right benchmark for a build partner, but it helps define the floor. Carta reports that the median pre-seed advisor grant was about 0.21%, and only a small minority of advisors received 1%+. Fractional CTO market guides commonly place hybrid cash/equity arrangements around 0.25%-2%, depending on time commitment and operating accountability.

Product-studio and service-for-equity examples are materially higher. Public examples show 5%-15% or 10%-20% equity for MVP/product build partnerships, especially when cash is reduced. True venture studios are higher still, often 30%-70% in ventures they create from scratch, because they supply capital, team, and company formation.

Looheru is not a studio-created company from scratch. Tamara brings founder-market fit, story, distribution, and a working field-tested prototype. Utlyze/SolutionStream brings the ability to turn that into a durable AI product/company. That makes the recommended fair zone lower than a full studio stake but higher than advisor equity.

Recommended Proposal Language

We recommend Option B: a Strategic Build Partner structure. It gives Tamara founder control and majority ownership, while giving Utlyze/SolutionStream real upside for taking product, architecture, implementation, AI voice, and operating risk. The equity should be committed up front but earned through vesting and milestones, not handed over fully vested on day one.
