Prompt 3: Counsel-Ready AI Voice, Healthcare, And Data Risk Plan

Act as a product counsel issue spotter, AI voice compliance researcher, healthcare privacy product manager, and risk-gated product strategist. This is not legal advice; the output should prepare the team and outside counsel.

Use the attached context zip first. It contains the Looheru research packet, the current founder-facing strategy report, prior GPT 5.5 Pro research outputs, the public family-coordination-assistant repo context, and the draft official proposal.

Context:
Voice AI is strategically important because parents identified calls, hold times, and phone tag as core pain. But the current recommendation is not to promise autonomous healthcare voice in the first paid MVP. First launch should focus on saved-contact SMS/email coordination and keep voice in a designed, gated, counsel-reviewed lane.

Research goal:
Increase confidence on what can be said in the official proposal about AI voice, healthcare scheduling, consent, data retention, and technical blocks.

Please produce:
1. Executive risk recommendation.
2. What is safe enough for MVP:
   - nonclinical voice
   - business main lines
   - no recording
   - no raw transcript retention
   - immediate AI disclosure
   - office opt-out
3. What should be human-reviewed:
   - healthcare scheduling
   - therapy/behavioral health
   - special-needs service coordination
   - identity verification
   - anything with sensitive details
4. What should be blocked:
   - clinical triage
   - diagnosis/treatment discussion
   - medications
   - insurance/billing
   - prior authorization
   - teen/minor confidential services
   - PHI-bearing provider-side workflows
5. Federal issues:
   - TCPA artificial/prerecorded voice
   - wireless/mobile line consent
   - FTC telemarketing versus informational calls
   - consumer health data and FTC Health Breach Notification Rule
   - HIPAA consumer-side versus provider-side/business associate boundary
6. State issues:
   - AI disclosure
   - call recording
   - transcription/STT treatment
   - all-party consent states
   - Utah/California/Colorado relevance
   - what needs a 50-state counsel memo
7. Product requirements:
   - disclosure language
   - opt-out
   - no-audio-recording setting
   - transcript deletion
   - structured-only extraction
   - line-type verification
   - human takeover triggers
   - prohibited-data filters
   - audit log without sensitive content
8. Vendor questions:
   - Twilio
   - voice AI provider
   - LLM provider
   - email provider
   - hosting/database
   - BAAs or HIPAA eligibility if future scope changes
9. Engineering acceptance criteria for a voice-safe MVP.
10. Proposal-ready language that explains the opportunity and the boundary without sounding fearful.
11. Questions to send to outside counsel.
