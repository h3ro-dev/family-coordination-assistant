Purpose

This pass answers James's request that the Looheru proposal show what supports each cited number, price pathway, percentage, and strategic determination. The proposal now uses compact citation chips and two appendix pages:

  • Source Register: source IDs for private evidence, repo evidence, market data, vendor pricing, legal/product risk sources, and ownership/equity benchmarks.
  • Calculation Notes: the method used to translate those sources into Looheru-specific planning ranges.

The intent is not to make the proposal look academic. The intent is to make the business logic auditable: Tamara should be able to see which numbers are external market anchors, which are vendor pass-through rates, which are direct Looheru evidence, and which are judgment calls that still require diligence.

Calculation Logic

Partner Option Pricing

The proposal does not treat the A/B/C paths as arbitrary quotes. Each path starts from market anchors and then adjusts for scope, AI-assisted delivery, equity, and responsibility.

PathFormula
Option A: Cash ServicesCustom software / SaaS MVP anchor, reduced for existing repo and AI-assisted development, no equity discount.
Option B: Strategic Build PartnerOption A cash range discounted because the partner receives earned upside; equity is above advisor grants and below true studio/cofounder economics.
Option C: Studio / Cofounder BuildCash can fall toward zero only if equity rises and the partner carries sustained team, subsidy, product leadership, operating, hiring/fundraising, and scale responsibility.

Consumer Pricing

The $29, $59, and $119 pilot tiers combine:

  • workflow/outcome-based AI pricing logic;
  • loaded COGS estimates by task type;
  • Stripe transaction drag on small monthly payments;
  • pilot learnings needed around willingness to pay, activation, repeat use, and relief;
  • guardrails against unlimited complex tasks, provider voice, or hidden concierge labor.

The consumer tiers should be treated as pilot hypotheses until paid conversion and real COGS are measured.

COGS

The COGS values in the proposal are loaded completed-task planning estimates, not raw vendor pass-through.

Formula:

loaded task COGS = vendor pass-through + payment allocation + AI/model calls + retry/failure buffer + complexity reserve

Implications:

  • SMS/email tasks have very low raw telecom/email costs, but still need a buffer for retries, parsing, failed replies, and support risk.
  • Voice tasks add Twilio call minutes, line-type lookup, AI/audio model cost, and a higher failure/retry reserve.
  • Complex browser/provider tasks are not expensive because of telecom; they are expensive because ambiguity, exceptions, compliance sensitivity, and failed automation create operating risk.

Pilot Size And Thresholds

The 30-40 invite and 18-24 enrolled-household range is a controlled first-pilot sample, not a statistical significance claim. It is meant to expose:

  • whether warm-network users will pay;
  • whether onboarding can stay autonomous;
  • whether at least one repeatable task loop works;
  • whether COGS stay inside plan economics;
  • whether the sponsor story has enough proof to become credible.

Activation and repeat-use thresholds are decision gates:

  • 70%+ setup activation means the self-serve onboarding flow is probably not too heavy.
  • 60%+ first-week task completion means the first loop is operationally viable.
  • 50%+ repeat within 30 days suggests the product is not only a novelty.

Sponsored Cohort Ranges

Sponsored ranges are planning models based on cohort size and implied per-member pilot price:

BuyerPlanning formula
Physician association25-50 members x $400-$500
Health-system workforce group50-100 employees x $400-$500
Employer benefit buyer100-150 employees x $300-$500
Women's leadership community25-60 members x $400-$500
Concierge community10-20 households x $750-$1,500

Those ranges should not be sold before B2C proof exists. They are designed as post-pilot sponsor conversation anchors.

Diligence Boundaries

These are planning ranges, not final SOW terms. Before signature, the team still needs:

  • repo/deployment/account access;
  • current production URL and usage state;
  • entity/IP/account ownership review;
  • actual scope lock for the first 6-8 weeks;
  • legal review of the equity/warrant instrument and vesting/repurchase terms;
  • counsel validation before voice scale, PHI-heavy workflow, teen/minor-confidential category, or state expansion.
