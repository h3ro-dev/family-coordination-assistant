Targeted GPT 5.5 Research Integration

Date: 2026-05-11

Executive Integration

The three targeted GPT 5.5 Pro reports materially increase confidence in the proposal path. They do not change the core recommendation. They make it sharper:

  1. Start founder-controlled and vendor-first. Looheru should not open with venture-studio equity, co-founder economics, or an equity-only build. The right next step is a paid 30-60 day Build + Own Sprint that preserves Tamara's control while cleaning up IP, account ownership, data ownership, and production responsibility.
  2. Run a paid June pilot, not a free beta. The pilot should enroll 18-24 high-burden households, stay narrowly focused on saved-contact babysitter/caregiver coordination over SMS/email, and test payment, repeat usage, COGS, support load, and emotional relief.
  3. Keep voice as a gated roadmap capability. Voice remains strategically important, but autonomous healthcare voice should not be in the first paid promise. Any MVP voice lane should be nonclinical, disclosed, business-main-line only, no audio recording, no raw transcript retention, structured-output only, and counsel-reviewed before scale.

Ownership Integration

The ownership report strengthens the founder-protective recommendation. The proposal should say plainly that Looheru should be built under a paid, founder-controlled services structure for the next 90 days. Tamara should keep majority ownership, CEO decision rights, board/control authority, and ownership of core accounts, venture-specific IP, customer data, analytics, domains, phone numbers, hosting, payment rails, prompts, workflows, and brand assets.

The report also makes the partnership economics more specific:

RelationshipRecommendation
Cash-only Build + Own SprintBest default now. Clean, fast, lowest ambiguity.
Cash plus small warrantPossible only if cash is materially discounted and milestones are clear.
Fractional CTO / product partnerLater, if the sprint and pilot prove a real ongoing role; should vest over time.
Venture studioToo early now; only revisit after pilot proof and clean ownership.
Revenue sharePossible for fee discounts, but must be capped and sunset.
Equity-only buildDo not offer now. It misprices risk and creates avoidable ownership ambiguity.

Suggested ranges to carry forward:

  • Advisor equity: normally 0.10%-0.25%, exceptional tightly scoped advisor 0.25%-0.50%.
  • Fractional CTO/product equity: generally 0.25%-1.00% with real cash compensation; stretch higher only for embedded multi-quarter responsibility.
  • Venture studio equity: 0% now; later consider 5%-12% earned in tranches if the studio adds sustained team, capital, and operating responsibility.
  • Warrant: 0.25%-1.00% only if tied to a discounted sprint, strategic relationship, or commercial milestone.
  • Revenue share: 2%-8% of net pilot revenue, capped around 1.25x-2.0x of foregone fees and sunset after 12-18 months.

Equity should wait until entity status, employment/IP conflicts, contributor assignments, repo ownership, production accounts, and offboarding mechanics are clean.

Sponsored cohorts should come after B2C evidence and be sold as narrow relief programs, not enterprise software rollouts:

  • Physician association: $10,000-$25,000 for 25-50 households.
  • Health-system workforce group: $20,000-$50,000 for 50-100 households.
  • Employer benefit buyer: $30,000-$75,000 only after stronger proof.
  • Women's leadership / professional community: $10,000-$30,000.
  • Concierge or high-income community: $15,000-$30,000.

Voice / Compliance Integration

The voice report validates the current caution and gives clearer product rules. The proposal should not make autonomous healthcare voice a launch promise. It can say voice is a strategic roadmap lane and that the sprint will prepare voice safely.

The MVP-safe voice posture is:

  • nonclinical logistics only;
  • verified business main lines only;
  • immediate AI disclosure;
  • office opt-out;
  • no voicemail drops;
  • no audio recording;
  • no durable raw transcript retention;
  • structured-only extraction;
  • line-type verification before calls;
  • prohibited-topic filters;
  • human takeover triggers;
  • audit log without sensitive content.

Human review is required for healthcare scheduling that may involve patient-specific facts, therapy/behavioral health, special-needs services, identity or authority verification, and anything likely to surface sensitive content.

The blocked lane should include clinical triage, diagnosis, treatment, medications, insurance, billing, prior authorization, teen/minor confidential services, provider-side PHI-bearing workflows, and any call requiring identity or personal-representative verification.

Outside counsel should review TCPA, AI disclosure, call recording/live transcription, FTC consumer-health-data rules, HIPAA consumer-side versus provider-side boundaries, state mini-TCPA/state privacy issues, and minor confidentiality rules before voice expands beyond a narrow, nonclinical pilot.
