SOW Cost Breakdown By Tranche

Plain read

Tamara is asking for the right thing: not just "what is the option," but what each option actually covers.

The cleanest way to show this is to use Option A as the baseline. Option A has no equity. That means it shows the market cash value of the work.

Then Options B and C become easier to understand:

  • Option A: Looheru pays cash. Looheru keeps all equity.
  • Option B: Looheru pays less cash. Utlyze / SolutionStream earns 10%-15% equity or warrants for taking part of the build risk.
  • Option C: Utlyze / SolutionStream builds at cost and takes 35%-40% ownership. The low-end Option A value becomes the value we are covering or putting at risk.

This should be written as a scope of work, not as a vague partnership idea.

Option B: strategic build partner

Option B should be a real shared-risk structure, not a vague discount.

Suggested structure:

  • Looheru pays part of the cash cost.
  • Utlyze / SolutionStream covers the remaining value through reduced fees.
  • In exchange, Utlyze / SolutionStream earns 10%-15% equity or warrants.
  • Equity should vest by milestones or time.
  • Unvested equity should stop if the relationship ends.

Option C: studio / cofounder build at cost

Option C is the deepest partnership.

This is not "cheap services." It is a company-building structure.

Suggested structure:

  • Utlyze / SolutionStream builds at cost.
  • Utlyze / SolutionStream receives 35%-40% ownership.
  • The low-end Option A baseline shows the market value covered.
  • Direct vendor/API/engineering cost is tracked separately.
  • First revenue pays direct product COGS.
  • After COGS, revenue repays approved direct build-cost recovery.
  • After recovery, revenue split follows the operating agreement.

Option C revenue order

Recommended order:

  1. Gross revenue comes into the company.
  2. Direct COGS is paid first. This includes live API usage, telecom, hosting, payment fees, and other per-task costs.
  3. Approved build-cost recovery is paid next until the ledger is repaid.
  4. After recovery, revenue split follows the operating agreement.
  5. Equity ownership remains governed by the operating agreement, not by monthly revenue alone.

What should be included in the final SOW

The signed SOW should include:

  • selected option,
  • tranche being authorized,
  • deliverables,
  • acceptance criteria,
  • rates,
  • vendor/API pass-through rules,
  • cost-recovery rules if Option C is selected,
  • what is excluded,
  • change-control process,
  • IP ownership,
  • background IP schedule,
  • account ownership checklist,
  • data and privacy boundaries,
  • counsel review items,
  • signature and start date.

Exclusions unless separately scoped

These should not be assumed inside the low-end tranche pricing:

  • full mobile app build,
  • broad consumer launch,
  • employer benefit sales program,
  • HIPAA-regulated clinical workflow,
  • clinical advice or diagnosis,
  • full two-sided camp marketplace,
  • nationwide provider/camp database,
  • 24/7 human support,
  • formal legal services,
  • full SOC 2 or enterprise security program,
  • fundraising materials beyond agreed support.
