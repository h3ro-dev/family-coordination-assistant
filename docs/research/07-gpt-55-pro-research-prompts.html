Working Context To Reuse In Prompts

Use this short context block at the top of any prompt where helpful:

Context:
We are evaluating Looheru, a proposed parent assistant / task execution intervention for high-load families. The product starts as an SMS-first family coordination assistant: a parent texts one number, the system contacts saved sitters or providers, aggregates availability, returns options, and confirms the selected option. It is not intended to be a marketplace, family calendar, reminder app, or spouse-nudging tool. The core wedge is reducing cognitive burden by actually completing coordination tasks.

Initial ICP hypothesis: high-performing working mothers, physician mothers, high-income dual-career households, and families with high coordination burden. Founder has healthcare AI/informatics credibility and access to physician-mother networks, but the first wedge should stay parent/caregiver-focused rather than clinical healthtech.

Known technical baseline: public repo at https://github.com/h3ro-dev/family-coordination-assistant. It implements a Phase 1 MVP with SMS/email coordination, saved contacts, admin UI, Postgres workflow state, Twilio/Resend integrations, background jobs, and a voice-result/Twilio Voice path.

We need to prepare for a follow-up meeting with actionable monetization options, partnership options, ICP/price sensitivity research, COGS modeling, competitive analysis, technical risk, and pilot design.

[Paste Working Context here.]

Build a first-pass COGS and unit economics model for a parent assistant that performs coordination tasks.

Model these workflows separately:
1. Babysitter coordination by SMS only.
2. Babysitter coordination by SMS + email.
3. Activity/summer camp coordination with browser/email work.

2. Task-credit structure.
3. Minimum viable price.
4. Metrics we must instrument in the MVP.
5. What assumptions matter most and need real pilot data.

Prompt 6: AI Voice Calls, Consent, And Compliance

Act as a legal/compliance research analyst and AI product risk analyst. This is research, not legal advice.

Use current primary sources where possible: FCC, FTC, TCPA guidance, state AI disclosure laws, call recording consent law summaries from reputable legal sources, HIPAA/HHS guidance, Twilio/provider docs, and official state materials. Cite sources with links and dates.

[Paste Working Context here.]

Research the legal/compliance constraints for a parent-authorized AI assistant that calls businesses, caregivers, camps, clinics, dentists, therapists, or healthcare offices on behalf of a parent to schedule or gather availability.

Focus on:
1. Is this different from robocalling or solicitation if the call is parent-authorized and task-specific?
2. What disclosure is required if an AI voice agent is interactive?
3. Which U.S. states have AI disclosure, call recording, two-party consent, or automated-call constraints that matter?
4. What changes when the call is to a healthcare provider or involves therapy/special-needs scheduling?
5. HIPAA boundary: when is this consumer family logistics versus PHI/HIPAA-adjacent or covered workflow?
6. What data should not be stored in early pilots?
7. How should deterministic logic be used to reduce risk?
8. What consent and audit logs should the product maintain?
9. What should the AI voice agent say at the start of a call?
10. What should remain human-reviewed until legal risk is clearer?

End with:
- launch-safe recommendations
- red/yellow/green workflow classification
- required counsel review questions
- exact product guardrails for the MVP
