## Source

James reviewed the public proposal on May 12, 2026 and flagged that the reading level still felt too high. The direction was to make the review feel easy for all audiences because ease of review affects emotional confidence in the proposal.

## Required changes

- Lower the reading level across the proposal, not only the first screen.
- Make the digestion process feel easier.
- Preserve the actual business meaning: pricing, equity, scope, risk gates, source support, legal caveats, and partnership options.
- Keep precise terms where needed, but add or improve plain-English layers.

## Proposal implementation

- Simplified the hero promise and executive summary.
- Rewrote dense headings in plainer language.
- Simplified confidence, positioning, pricing, pilot, sponsored cohort, COGS, sprint, options, scope, market, risk gate, investment, next-step, source, and calculation copy.
- Added or strengthened plain-English blocks before dense decision areas.
- Kept the public source register and calculation notes intact, but made their lead language clearer and easier to skim.
