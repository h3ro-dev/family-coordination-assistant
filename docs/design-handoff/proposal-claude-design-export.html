Chapter05
Pricing

Price the pilot for completed tasks, not another app.

Section 05 / 17

The June pilot should be paid, narrow, and easy to explain. The first job is to learn whether families pay, start using it, use it again, and feel relief. B1G1COGS

Essential

$29/30d
Entry paid plan for occasional but recurring coordination.
  • 4 in-scope tasks.
  • Up to 5 saved contacts per task.
  • Saved-contact SMS/email.
  • One options summary.
  • One confirmation loop.
  • Autonomous guided setup.
  • No autonomous healthcare calls.
Basis. Four simple tasks should leave margin at $29 after Stripe. This tests paid demand without promising unlimited work. V3COGS

Plus

$59/30d
Best balance of value, usage, and margin.
  • 10 in-scope tasks.
  • Simple coordination = 1 credit.
  • Complex tasks = 2+ credits.
  • Autonomous contact/calendar setup.
  • Broader saved-contact lists.
Basis. Ten credits gives families enough use to feel value. Complex tasks use more credits to protect margin. B1COGS

Signature

$119/30d
Premium reliability for high-burden households.
  • 20 in-scope tasks.
  • Priority automation queue.
  • Richer household/contact graph.
  • Limited parent-facing voice intake beta.
  • Provider calls excluded from pilot.
Basis. This tier prices priority, richer setup, and parent voice intake without taking on provider-call risk. V2L1

Chapter13
Risk Gates

Move voice forward, but use clear gates.

Section 13 / 17

New voice-agent tools and the current Twilio Voice work make voice worth testing sooner. Provider calls still carry legal and operating risk. That risk should create clear gates, not stop the whole voice path. V2V1L1

Plain read: use voice where risk is low. Slow down where it starts to sound like healthcare, billing, or sensitive family data.
Green

Safe first workflows

Babysitters, saved caregivers, household services, and nonclinical coordination. Start with text and email. L1V1

Yellow

Test carefully before scale

Provider scheduling, therapy, behavioral health, special-needs services, identity checks, and anything likely to reveal sensitive details. L2L3

Red

Do not automate in MVP

Do not automate clinical advice, diagnosis, treatment, medication, insurance, billing, prior authorization, teen confidential services, provider-side PHI workflows, saved call transcripts, or healthcare voice without written controls and counsel review before scale. L2L3L4

Launch-safe voice rule

If voice is used early, start with parent onboarding and task intake. Then test scripted, low-risk logistics calls on the current Twilio Voice base. Outbound business calls should be nonclinical, disclosed, not recorded, not saved as raw transcripts, limited to structured results, line-type checked, business-main-line only, and easy for offices to opt out or send the parent back to call. L1V1L4
