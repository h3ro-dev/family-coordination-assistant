Chapter05
Pricing

Price the paid pilot for completed coordination, not passive organization.

Section 05 / 17

The June pilot should be paid, tightly scoped, and easy to understand. These can become recurring packages after proof, but the first job is to test payment, activation, repeat usage, COGS, and relief. B1G1COGS

Essential

$29/30d
Entry paid plan for occasional but recurring coordination.
  • 4 in-scope tasks.
  • Up to 5 saved contacts per task.
  • Saved-contact SMS/email.
  • One options summary.
  • One confirmation loop.
  • Autonomous guided setup.
  • No autonomous healthcare calls.
Basis. 4 tasks × low-SMS COGS leaves margin at $29 after Stripe; entry anchor tests paid demand without unlimited work. V3COGS

Plus

$59/30d
Best balance of value, usage, and margin.
  • 10 in-scope tasks.
  • Simple coordination = 1 credit.
  • Complex tasks = 2+ credits.
  • Autonomous contact/calendar setup.
  • Broader saved-contact lists.
Basis. 10-credit midpoint uses workflow/outcome pricing while protecting against complex tasks with 2+ credit rules. B1COGS

Signature

$119/30d
Premium reliability for high-burden households.
  • 20 in-scope tasks.
  • Priority automation queue.
  • Richer household/contact graph.
  • Limited parent-facing voice intake beta.
  • Provider calls excluded from pilot.
Basis. Premium tier prices priority, richer setup data, and voice-intake beta separately from provider-call risk. V2L1

Chapter13
Risk Gates

Voice AI should move earlier under legal-informed product gates.

Section 13 / 17

The May 2026 voice-agent releases and the existing Twilio Voice implementation support a more ambitious roadmap. The legal and operational issues around provider calls remain real, but they should become explicit product gates rather than a reason to freeze the voice path. V2V1L1

Green

Safe first workflows

Babysitter coordination, saved-contact caregiver outreach, household services, and nonclinical coordination with SMS/email first. L1V1

Yellow

Research-gated / counsel-validated before scale

Provider scheduling, therapy, behavioral health, special-needs services, identity or authority verification, and any interaction likely to surface sensitive details. L2L3

Red

Do not automate in MVP

Clinical triage, diagnosis, treatment, medication, insurance, billing, prior authorization, teen/minor confidential services, provider-side PHI workflows, durable call transcripts, or healthcare voice without documented controls and counsel validation before scale. L2L3L4

Launch-safe voice rule

If voice is used early, start with parent-facing onboarding and task intake, then scripted low-risk outbound logistics using the current Twilio Voice foundation. For outbound business calls: nonclinical, disclosed, no voicemail drops, no audio recording, no raw transcript retention, structured-output only, line-type verified, business-main-line only, and easy for offices to opt out or ask the parent to call back. L1V1L4
