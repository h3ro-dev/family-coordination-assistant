Executive Summary01

Our recommendation: a 30-60 day Build + Own Sprint under a strategic build-partner structure.

The strongest version of Looheru is not another family calendar, parent reminder app, childcare marketplace, or generic AI assistant. The strongest version is an execution layer for overloaded family operators: one request from the parent, outreach to the right real-world people, options returned, decision confirmed, and the loop closed.

Build

Harden the current MVP into a chargeable pilot product with autonomous onboarding, payments, security, calendar sync, observability, and task-level instrumentation.

Own

Clean up entity, IP, domain, repo, phone, hosting, email, data, contributor, and credential ownership before anyone accepts production responsibility.

Grow

Use the first paid cohort to prove repeat use, willingness to pay, COGS, autonomous containment, and voice-lane safety before expanding into sponsors or deeper studio economics.

The recommended path is a market-grounded strategic build partner structure: $50K-$90K sprint cash, $15K-$30K/month if continued, and 5%-10% earned equity or warrants.

Risk Gates13

Voice AI should move earlier under legal-informed product gates.

The May 2026 voice-agent releases and the existing Twilio Voice implementation support a more ambitious roadmap. The legal and operational issues around provider calls remain real, but they should become explicit product gates rather than a reason to freeze the voice path.

Green

Safe first workflows

Babysitter coordination, saved-contact caregiver outreach, household services, and nonclinical coordination with SMS/email first.

Yellow

Research-gated / counsel-validated before scale

Provider scheduling, therapy, behavioral health, special-needs services, identity or authority verification, and any interaction likely to surface sensitive details.

Red

Do not automate in MVP

Clinical triage, diagnosis, treatment, medication, insurance, billing, prior authorization, teen/minor confidential services, provider-side PHI workflows, durable call transcripts, or healthcare voice without documented controls and counsel validation before scale.

Launch-safe voice rule

If voice is used early, start with parent-facing onboarding and task intake, then scripted low-risk outbound logistics using the current Twilio Voice foundation. For outbound business calls: nonclinical, disclosed, no voicemail drops, no audio recording, no raw transcript retention, structured-output only, line-type verified, business-main-line only, and easy for offices to opt out or ask the parent to call back.

Voice and healthcare risk gatesMove intake earlier, validate scale gates

Research questions to refine before signature

What is already production-ready? What is the first 6-8 week scope? What 90-day cash budget is available? What dilution is acceptable before paid pilot proof? Which voice lanes are in phase one? Who owns incidents, refunds, support, vendor accounts, and safety tradeoffs?

The near-term goal is not to build everything. The near-term goal is to prove the first repeatable promise.

No-go lines: no fully vested equity without scope and vesting, no free beta as the main proof, no healthcare/provider voice before documented controls and counsel validation for scale, and no broad family autopilot before the first coordination loop is paid and repeatable.
